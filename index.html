World conditions


The world is 8× wider and 8× taller than the viewport (64× the area), with an 800-cell population cap. Drag or swipe the world, hold WASD for smooth camera movement, or use the mobile camera pad. Scroll or use the zoom buttons to zoom in and out. The camera stops at world edges. Starts with exactly one cell. It must find food and build enough energy to reproduce. Each cell has 10 distinct genes; mutations can create new combinations and species. Connect-gene cells can form locked multicellular colonies after each cell is at least 3 seconds old, share food energy with the hungriest member, and attract compatible cells across species. Connect and Herding genes are more common. Herding cells coordinate food access within their own species. Chaser cells follow nearby cells; following loops elect the highest-energy leader, and aggressive Chasers move slightly faster. There is no automatic population rescue: if the lineage dies out, restart to try a new evolutionary run.

03 / Species explorer

A lineage becomes a new species when 50% or more of its ten genes differ from the founding genome of its current species (at least 5 genes). Species records remain after extinction.
